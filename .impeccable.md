# Project: mitej.dev — personal site

Single-page-app personal site for Mitej Madan, full stack engineer at TheAgentic (Mumbai).

## Design Context

### Users
Three audiences, in priority order:

1. **Hiring managers and engineering leads** skimming for signal in under 60 seconds. They want to know what he builds, how deep it goes, and whether the craft is real. The site itself is the primary evidence — if it loads instantly and feels precise, that argues better than any adjective.
2. **Peers and collaborators** who arrive from GitHub, X, or a link in a DM. They want the project list and the code behind it.
3. **Recruiters and founders** who need the fast facts: what he does, where he is, how to reach him.

The context is almost always a link opened in a new tab between other tasks. Nobody arrives intending to read. The job to be done is *fast, confident evaluation* — so nothing may sit between the viewer and the content, and the first paint must already say who this is.

### Brand Personality
**Precise · understated · engineered.**

The voice is first-person, plain, and specific. It names systems and numbers rather than reaching for adjectives — "email campaign automation with scheduled sends and per-recipient tracking," not "passionate about building scalable solutions." No exclamation marks, no hype vocabulary, no "I'm on a mission to." Lowercase is fine in metadata, but sentences are properly cased — this is quiet, not casual.

The emotional target is **calm confidence**. The reader should feel they are looking at the work of someone with nothing to prove and therefore no need to shout.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Refined minimalism, executed with restraint.** The whole site is one narrow measure of text on a near-white ground, with a single warm accent used sparingly enough that each appearance means something.

- **Reference**: `modimihir.com` — a colleague's site, and the structural template here. Match its bones exactly: a ~640px single column, generous vertical rhythm, hairline rules separating list rows instead of cards, a glass nav that floats, section labels as small quiet kickers, scroll-reveal on entry, pre-paint theme resolution.
- **Reference**: `hrdkk.com` — for its warmth and directness in copy, and its compact stacked-row treatment of work history and projects.
- **Deliberate divergence**: Mihir is at the same company, so an element-for-element clone would produce two near-identical sites. Same system and craft, different identity: **Instrument Sans** rather than Archivo, and a warm **ember** accent rather than his blue. Monospace appears only for tabular metadata (year columns), never as a costume for "technical."
- **Anti-references**: developer-portfolio templates with hero gradients and glowing cards; dark-mode-with-neon-cyan; card grids of identical tiles; big animated hero numbers; "Let's build something amazing together" CTAs; anything with a particle background.
- **Theme**: light by default, dark available via toggle, resolved before first paint so there is never a flash.

### Design Principles

1. **Load before it can be doubted.** Speed is a design feature, not an optimization phase. No animation library, no router library, no icon package, no web font blocking paint. If a dependency costs more than the value it adds to the reader, it does not ship.
2. **One column, one voice.** Everything lives in a single narrow measure. Hierarchy comes from type scale, weight, and space — never from boxes, shadows, or borders drawn around things. Hairline rules divide; cards do not contain.
3. **The accent is a budget.** Ember appears on interactive affordances and almost nowhere else. Spending it everywhere would make it mean nothing.
4. **Motion clarifies arrival, then gets out of the way.** One choreographed entrance and content revealing as it enters view. Exponential ease-out only, no bounce, transform and opacity only, and a genuine static fallback under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Every word is load-bearing.** No section restates its own heading. No sentence exists to fill space. If a line could be deleted without loss, it is deleted.
