## Complete UI Book Styles Pack

The reading book — cover, both page sprites, arrow and cross icons.

- **By:** Crusenho Agus Hennihuno
- **Store:** https://crusenho.itch.io
- **Product:** https://crusenho.itch.io/complete-ui-book-styles-pack
- **Shipped as:** `public/game/book/{cover,page-left,page-right,arrow,cross}.png`

Free for personal and commercial use, and free to adapt. Two conditions worth
stating plainly, because both bind us:

> "Give appropriate credit, or provide a link to this product page, and indicate
> if changes were made."

Credit and the product link are given here and in-product on the overworld
screen. **Changes made:** sprites are used unmodified; they are composed
programmatically (cover with the two pages inset) and scaled with
`image-rendering: pixelated`. No pixels were altered.

This repository is public, which makes that clause worth naming: the sprites
ship as part of a working site, not as a redistributable asset pack, and are not
offered for download or resale. Nothing here is an NFT.

## Not used

`~/Desktop/pok3` is a modular character *generator* (style `pokemon-gen-3`), not a
sprite sheet — its base layers are palette keys with no head, face or clothes, and
composing them needs the desktop tool that produced it. Nothing from it ships, so
its licence has not been reviewed. If a custom character is ever exported from it,
check that licence first.
